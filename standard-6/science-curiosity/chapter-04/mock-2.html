Level: Medium to hard Marks: 25 Time: 45 minutes
  • All questions are compulsory.
  • The marks for each question are shown against it.
  • In Section A choose the one correct option. In the other sections write complete sentences.

Section A: Multiple choice questions 6 x 1 = 6 marks

  1. [1]

    A bar magnet is broken into three pieces. How many poles do the three pieces have in all?

    1. (a) 2
    2. (b) 3
    3. (c) 4
    4. (d) 6
    Answer
  2. [1]

    An iron bar is hung freely from a thread and turned gently several times. Each time, it will

    1. (a) rest along the north-south direction
    2. (b) rest along the east-west direction
    3. (c) rest along any direction
    4. (d) keep on rotating
    Answer
  3. [1]

    The North pole of a bar magnet is slowly brought near the North pole of a compass needle. The North pole of the needle

    1. (a) moves closer to the magnet
    2. (b) moves away from the magnet
    3. (c) does not move
    4. (d) starts spinning
    Answer
  4. [1]

    On some magnets the poles are not marked N and S. On such a magnet, the North pole may be shown by

    1. (a) a white dot
    2. (b) blue paint
    3. (c) a black line
    4. (d) a small hole
    Answer
  5. [1]

    Assertion (A): An iron bar brought near a compass needle cannot push either end of the needle away.
    Reason (R): Repulsion takes place only between the like poles of two magnets.

    1. (a) Both A and R are true, and R explains A.
    2. (b) Both A and R are true, but R does not explain A.
    3. (c) A is true, but R is false.
    4. (d) A is false, but R is true.
    Answer
  6. [1]

    A strong magnet is brought near a coin. The coin is not attracted at all. Which conclusion is correct?

    1. (a) The coin is made of a non-magnetic material.
    2. (b) The coin is made of iron.
    3. (c) The magnet has lost one of its poles.
    4. (d) The coin is a magnet.
    Answer
