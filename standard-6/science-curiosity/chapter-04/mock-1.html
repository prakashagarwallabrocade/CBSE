Level: Easy to medium Marks: 25 Time: 45 minutes

Section A: Multiple choice questions 6 x 1 = 6 marks

  1. [1]

    Which of these is a magnetic material?

    1. (a) Nickel
    2. (b) Wood
    3. (c) Glass
    4. (d) Plastic
    Answer
  2. [1]

    Magnets that are found in nature are called

    1. (a) artificial magnets
    2. (b) lodestones
    3. (c) compasses
    4. (d) iron filings
    Answer
  3. [1]

    A bar magnet is placed on a heap of iron filings and lifted. Where do most of the filings stick?

    1. (a) In the middle of the magnet
    2. (b) Equally all over the magnet
    3. (c) Near the two ends of the magnet
    4. (d) Only on one end of the magnet
    Answer
  4. [1]

    A bar magnet hangs freely from a thread. Along which direction does it come to rest?

    1. (a) Any direction
    2. (b) East-west
    3. (c) It never comes to rest
    4. (d) North-south
    Answer
  5. [1]

    The South pole of one magnet is brought near the South pole of another magnet. The two poles will

    1. (a) repel each other
    2. (b) attract each other
    3. (c) not affect each other
    4. (d) first attract and then repel
    Answer
  6. [1]

    Assertion (A): A magnet can move a compass needle even when a sheet of cardboard is kept between them.
    Reason (R): The magnetic effect can act through non-magnetic materials.

    1. (a) Both A and R are true, and R explains A.
    2. (b) Both A and R are true, but R does not explain A.
    3. (c) A is true, but R is false.
    4. (d) A is false, but R is true.
    Answer
